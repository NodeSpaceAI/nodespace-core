Call search_nodes with node_type "person" and the name as query to find the person who owes the answer; when nobody is named, it is the creator of the stopped task. Call create_node with node_type "task" and the question as content, then create_relationship with from_id the person's id, relationship_type "tasks" and to_id the question's id, so it is assigned to them. Link it to the stopped task's project: call get_node_context with the stopped task's id to read its project, then create_relationship with from_id the project's id, relationship_type "tasks" and to_id the question's id. Then call create_relationship with from_id the question's id, relationship_type "blocks" and to_id the stopped task's id. Leave the stopped task's status and assignee as they are.
