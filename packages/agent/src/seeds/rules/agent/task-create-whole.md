Call create_task once, with title the task's title, brief the paragraphs of its brief, checklist the conditions of its checklist (one condition each, with no checkbox marker), project the project's name or id when it belongs to one, and node_type "issue" for a change to code or "task" for anything else. That one call creates the task, writes its brief and its unticked checklist under it, and links it from its project, so it is ready the moment it exists. A new task is open. Do not build it from create_node and create_relationship, and make the call once, when the brief and the checklist are written.
