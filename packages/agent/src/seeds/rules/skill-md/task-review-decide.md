For each finding, run `nodespace node update <finding-id> --content "<its text> Decision: <fix, defer or reject>. Reason: <why>."`. A finding decided fix keeps `- [ ] ` at its start. A finding decided defer or reject has `- [ ] ` changed to `- [x] `, since nothing is left to do. A finding decided defer opens its reason with `size small`, `size medium` or `size large`.
