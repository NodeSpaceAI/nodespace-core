Use this to reach a shared understanding with the user by working through the open decisions with them, and to act on none of them until they have answered. It is for a request that is ambiguous, for a user who says they are not sure what they want or asks to think something through, and before a spec is written.

MAP THE OPEN DECISIONS: list every decision the work needs and what each one depends on, as a tree in which one decision opens others. The questions you can ask now are the ones whose prerequisites are already settled: that is the frontier. A question whose answer depends on one still open waits for a later round.

LOOK FACTS UP YOURSELF: a fact is yours to find, never the user's. Read what is there before asking for anything you could learn that way, and ask only for what only the user can decide. <!-- include: ask-look-up --> A lookup in progress is an unsettled prerequisite: ask the questions that do not wait on it.

ASK ONE QUESTION AT A TIME, the one on the frontier whose answer opens the most others. Put it as a short decision brief, in this order:

- The question, as a short title.
- What it is about, in plain language: what it changes for the user, in their own words, with no document, section or record numbers.
- What is at stake if the choice is wrong.
- At most four options, each with its main benefit and its main cost.
- Your recommendation and the reason for it, worded so that "yes" accepts it.

<!-- include: ask-decision-brief -->

A RECOMMENDATION IS NEVER AN ANSWER: do not act on it, and do not treat silence or a vague reply as agreement. Wait for the answer. If it does not choose, ask again more plainly.

AFTER EACH ANSWER, work the tree again: a settled decision opens the questions that hung off it, and may make others unnecessary. Say so rather than asking them anyway.

IF THE USER SAYS THEY DO NOT FOLLOW a question, stop asking and use the skill Explaining. Another question is the wrong answer to "I don't follow".

IT IS DONE WHEN THE FRONTIER IS EMPTY: every branch has been visited and nothing is silently assumed. Say back in a few lines what was agreed, and ask the user to confirm it before anyone acts on it.
